# 🌊 PRAVAAH

### Predictive Risk Assessment & Adaptive Assistance for Hazards

**Predict. Prepare. Protect.**

PRAVAAH is an AI-powered disaster intelligence and emergency response platform designed to identify high-risk regions, assess infrastructure vulnerability, and support safer evacuation and rescue planning through geospatial intelligence, machine learning, and what-if simulations.

## 🎯 Problem Statement

Disaster management often faces challenges in predicting localized risks, identifying vulnerable infrastructure, determining accessible evacuation routes, and planning emergency responses under changing conditions.

PRAVAAH aims to address these challenges through an integrated platform that transforms geographical and environmental data into actionable insights for disaster preparedness and response.

## 💡 Key Features

### 🔴 High-Risk Area Identification

* Estimate disaster risk using relevant environmental, geographical, and historical data.
* Visualize high-risk regions through interactive maps.
* Identify potentially vulnerable infrastructure and transportation routes.

### 🟢 Lower-Risk Area Identification

* Identify comparatively lower-risk regions for further evacuation assessment.
* Evaluate road accessibility and nearby emergency facilities.
* Support the identification of candidate shelters and evacuation destinations.

### 🔄 What-If Disaster Simulation

* Simulate hypothetical scenarios, such as increased rainfall or blocked roads.
* Analyze potential changes in risk zones and route accessibility.
* Compare baseline conditions with alternative scenarios to support emergency planning.

### 🗺️ Geospatial Decision Support

* Visualize geographical risk and infrastructure exposure.
* Assess route feasibility and hazard exposure.
* Support emergency prioritization and response planning.

## ⚙️ How It Works

```text
Environmental & Geographical Data
               ↓
      Data Processing
               ↓
       AI/ML Risk Assessment
               ↓
      Geospatial Intelligence
               ↓
   Route & Emergency Analysis
               ↓
      What-If Simulation
               ↓
     Interactive Dashboard
```

The platform is designed to combine predictive risk assessment with geographical analysis and scenario-based planning, helping decision-makers evaluate potential risks and compare response strategies.
